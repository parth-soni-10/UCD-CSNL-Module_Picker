Privacy

What this site keeps, and where

CSNL Module Picker has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts. Your timetable choices never leave your browser. This page lists the little data the site does handle, so nothing about it is a surprise.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

  • Cookies None
  • Analytics None
  • Accounts None
  • Server-side data Suggestion form only

What stays in your browser

Your selected modules, added module codes, named timetables, chosen theme and the last fetched timings are kept in your browser's localStorage, on your device. They are not sent to this site or to UCD. Clearing site data, or using a private window, removes them. An export is a JSON file you download yourself, so it lives wherever you put it.

What is stored on the server

The only user-submitted data is the suggestion form. When you send a suggestion, the message, an optional name and an optional email address are stored in this site's Cloudflare KV namespace so the maintainer can act on them. The store keeps at most the most recent 500 suggestions, and entries are removed as they are handled. Nothing else you do is written to a server — choosing modules, planning a timetable and exporting it are all local.

Requests to UCD

Class times and module pages are fetched by this site's own server-side proxy. Your browser does not contact UCD for that data, so UCD does not receive your IP address from those requests. Fonts, scripts and styles are loaded only from this domain. Following a link to UCD, Discord, GitHub or the maintainer's portfolio takes you to that service, which has its own privacy policy.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes requests and keeps standard operational logs — IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamp — as any web host does, for security and reliability. This project does not build profiles from those logs, combine them with anything else, or sell or share data. There is no third-party embed on any page, so no other company sees your visit through this site.

Cookies and consent

This site sets no cookies and runs no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to: no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no cross-site tracking. The service worker caches the app's own files on your device so it loads quickly; that cache contains no personal data and is cleared with the rest of the site's storage.

Your rights

If you have sent a suggestion and want it removed, or want to know what is held about it, email soni.soni.parth@gmail.com. Under the GDPR you may also ask for access, correction or erasure of personal data, and you have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission. If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Questions about this page, or about data in a suggestion you sent? See Contact.
